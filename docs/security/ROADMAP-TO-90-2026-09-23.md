## 2026-09-24 08:20 local: why collectors go stale

Measured, not assumed. Every collector gap in the last 40 hours coincides with
Windows itself pausing or restarting (System log, Kernel-Power 506/507 Modern
Standby, 42/107 sleep, 41/6008 unexpected shutdown, 13/12 restarts): Modern
Standby from 01:18 to 06:54 Windows time froze the WSL VM for 5.6 h. While the
VM is frozen nothing runs, and no keep-alive inside WSL can change that. The
guest's monotonic clock stops during the freeze (wall minus monotonic implied a
boot 4.3 h later than the real one).

Windows runs on UTC+2 and WSL on EEST (UTC+3); Windows log times are one hour
behind WSL times. Adjusted for that, the post-wake catch-up was quick: 60 s
collectors ran again within ~1.5 min and every overdue collector within
~10 min.

The score damage came from two JARVIS defects, both fixed in the checkout:

1. **No hold after resume.** The scheduler holds coverage and report after a
   process restart until every stale collector has run, but not after a
   suspend. Coverage ran at 07:56:40 mid-sweep, called 21 waiting collectors
   stale, and report recorded **10** (steady ~70). The same collapse appears
   after every gap in the history (11, 26, 28, 10). The scheduler now detects
   a resume (wall clock outrunning monotonic by at least 60 s) and re-arms the
   same bounded 15-minute hold. `reliability.resume` reports count, time and
   the collectors held, as evidence.
2. **Coverage verdicts outlived their evidence.** Under host load (CPU 143%,
   governor deferring maintenance) coverage itself was starved, so its
   07:56 verdicts kept Monitoring at 0 until about 08:17 although the
   collectors had recovered by 08:04. The scorer now ignores a coverage
   verdict only when its collector has a newer, error-free, in-window
   result. A still-stale or failing collector remains blind and its area
   scores 0.

The churn also inflates the alert ledger ("a resolved alert has come back",
13 highs) and pages. Existing ledger entries are history and stay.

Remaining, owner decision: the laptop sleeps after 45 min idle on AC and on
battery. Continuous monitoring overnight requires not sleeping on AC power.

The score is the rounded average of seven areas when coverage is complete. Each

required about 236 total area-points from the pre-deployment baseline, and
still requires about 184 from the post-release snapshot. It
cannot be achieved by a single setting or by re-labeling findings. Zero

## Security gates beyond this score

The score omits some failure modes and cannot represent a fully secured system.
For release, record current evidence and an owner for each of these gates:

| Gate | Evidence to capture | If absent |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Boot and platform integrity | Secure Boot, TPM, VBS/HVCI, driver-blocklist and firmware-update state from Windows, with timestamps | State unknown; inventory is not attestation |
| Account protection | MFA for important accounts, recovery methods, standard daily user, administrative elevation, session and token review | Credential theft may survive host hardening |
| Updates and application control | OS, browser, firmware and app update status; effective Smart App Control/AppLocker/WDAC and ASR modes, exclusions, and blocked-event history | Audit-only or missing policy is not prevention |
| Data survival | Offline or otherwise independently recoverable backup, restore drill, encrypted storage, recovery-key custody | Encryption alone cannot recover deleted or ransomware-encrypted data |
| Browser and downloads | Extension permissions, download origin and signature, safe navigation and a tested privacy-sensor deployment | Backend detection cannot prove browser-time protection |
| WSL and local boundaries | WSL/Hyper-V firewall state, root service code seal, sudo grants, mounts, loopback exposure, package startup hooks | Windows firewall alone does not close these paths |
| JARVIS trust | Assurance register, source/release integrity, native telemetry chain, vault/anchor verification, sustained health, rollback drill | A protection score can be high while the monitor is unreliable |

Record unknown states explicitly. Prior observations of Credential Guard, HVCI,
LSA protection, ASR and network protection need a fresh host read before they
can be treated as current. This checklist should be reviewed even if the score
reaches 90.
