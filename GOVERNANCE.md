# Source-preview governance

The upstream project maintainers are @chrisnkuno and @soniAineza4. Copyrights
and attribution remain with their holders. This repository is a source review
surface for the selected upstream commit; it does not establish upstream merge
approval, release authority, signing identity or operational security validation.

Maintainers review changes for scope, correctness, privacy, safety and licensing.
External contributions require the provenance and commercial-rights record in
CONTRIBUTING.md. AI judgments are advisory; tests and explicit human authority
remain decisive. Forks must follow the license covering their version and must
not imply official endorsement or reuse official signing credentials.

Synchronization with private development requires a new reviewed export with
an exact source commit and public file manifest. Never automatically mirror
private history, operational evidence, credentials, draft advisories or CI logs.
The upstream repository and its branch protections remain independent.
